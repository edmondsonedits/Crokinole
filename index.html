Quick rules

Opponent on board? Your shot must contact one of their discs. Otherwise, finish in or touching the 15 circle. Highest board score wins the round: 2 match points; a tie is 1–1.

Tap the board edge to place · drag back from your disc · release to shoot

How to play

This version follows the core World Crokinole Championship singles rules and uses eight discs per player each round.

Take a shot

  1. Tap your highlighted outer shooting arc to place a disc.
  2. Press the disc, drag backward like a slingshot, and release.
  3. Longer pull = more power.

Valid shots

  • If an opponent disc is on the board, one of your discs must contact an opponent disc during the shot.
  • If no opponent disc is in play, at least one of your involved discs must finish inside or touching the 15 circle, or score a 20.
  • On an invalid shot, the shooting disc and your same-colour discs involved in that shot are removed.

Scoring

A centre hole is 20 points. Remaining discs score 15, 10, or 5 by zone. A disc touching a scoring line receives the lower value. Each round winner earns 2 match points; a tie gives 1 point to each player. A match is four rounds.
